Flavoured Rice

Description

After moving from England to the US one of the things I noticed about Indian restaurants is that they only give you plain rice with curries, which kind of sucks. So this is an easy Indian flavoured rice that goes amazing with curry, and if you have left overs you can make an incredible Indian-style egg friend rice (amalgamation to the max!)

Ingredients

  • 1 cup basmati rice
  • 2 tbsp ghee or butter
  • 2 tsp turmeric powder
  • 1 tsp cumin seeds
  • 2 bay leaves
  • 4 cardamom pods
  • 1 tbsp garlic, minced
  • 1 tbsp ginger, grated (use frozen ginger!)
  • Salt to tatse

Instructions

Cook the rice according to the instructions on the packet! I am not a rice connoisseur (I used to cook it in any amount of water and then drain it...)

  1. Add butter, garlic, ginger, cumin seeds, bay leaves, and cardamom pods to a pan and sauté for a minute.
  2. Add the basmati rice and turmeric powder, and stir for a minute.
  3. Add water and salt, bring to a boil, then reduce heat and simmer for 15-20 minutes or until the rice is cooked.
