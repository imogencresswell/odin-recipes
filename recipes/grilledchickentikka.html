Grilled Chicken Tikka

Wrong recipe? Click here to return home!

Description

This is a simple grilled chicken tikka recipe that we like to cook on the bbq! The oven works too and if you're lucky enough to have a tandoor then absolutely use it (I'm jealous)! It's really case of making the marinade and then throwing it on the grill!

Ingredients

  • 4 large chicken breasts (slice 2 Costco chicken in half to make 4)
  • 1/2 cup plain yogurt
  • 2 tbsp lemon juice
  • 2 tbsp ginger, grated
  • 2 tbsp garlic, minced
  • 2 tsp cumin powder
  • 2 tsp coriander powder
  • 1 tsp turmeric powder
  • 1 tsp kasmiri chili powder
  • 1 tbsp garam masala
  • 2 tsp salt
  • 2 tbsp fenugreek powder
  • 2 tsp Cayenne pepper
  • 2 tsp ground black pepper
  • 2 tbsp avocado or mustard oil

Instructions

  1. Mix all the ingredients together in a bowl and then add the chicken and coat
  2. Cover and refrigerate for at least 2 hours, but overnight is best
  3. Preheat the grill to medium-high heat and then grill the chicken for 5-7 minutes on each side or until fully cooked
