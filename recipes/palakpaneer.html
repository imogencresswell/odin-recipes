Palak Paneer

Wrong recipe? Click here to return home!

Description

Palak paneer is one of my favourite Indian dishes to make and dare I say I've become pretty good at it! It's made with spinach and paneer and I've found it goes well with grilled chicken tikka. Note: it's different to saag paneer because it only contains spinach and not other greens (I actually learnt this from Swasthi's Recipes and this recipe is kind of an amalgamation of Saag Paneer and Palak Paneer).

Ingredients

Note: All the measurements are estimated here, I just measure with my heart!

For the Paneer:

  • 1lb Paneer cut into cubes (I use Costco's paneer, but it's pretty easy to make your own! Once again, check out the legend that is Swasthi's Recipes)
  • 2 tbsp oil (I use avocado oil but any oil with a highish smoke point will do)
  • 1 tbsp tumeric
  • 1 tsp salt
  • 2 tsp cayenne pepper (or however much spice you like)
  • 1 tsp fine ground black pepper

For the Palak (Spinach):

  • 1 tbsp oil(I use avocado oil but any oil with a highish smoke point will do, apparently mustard oil is traditional and gives a deeper flavour!)
  • 1 bag frozen spinach
  • 1 large onion, chopped
  • 2 Serrano or jalapeño, chopped (add more or less depending on spice level, keeping seeds in makes it spicier)
  • 1 large tomato, chopped
  • 5 large garlic cloves
  • 2 tbsp ginger, grated (freezing ginger makes it easier to grate!)
  • 2 tsp cumin seeds
  • 2 tsp salt (again, measure with your heart, I like it salty)
  • 1 tsp tumeric
  • 1 tbsp garam masala
  • 1 tbsp coriander powder
  • 2 tsp kashmiri chili powder
  • 1 tbsp fenugreek powder
  • Some bay leaves
  • Some cardomon pods
  • Some greek yoghurt or cream

Instructions

For the Paneer:

  1. Add 1 tbsp oil to a bowl with the spices and cubed paneer, mixing well.
  2. Let marinate for at least 30 mins (overnight is great!)
  3. Heat 1 tbsp oil in a large pan over medium to high heat.
  4. Add paneer and fry until a nice golden colour. It's easiest to do in batches, and you can be anal about it and rotate each cube to get it even, or just stir every 5 ish minutes.
  5. Set aside for later

For the Palak (Spinach):

  1. Heat 1 tbsp oil in a large pan over medium to high heat. If making a big batch a large dutch oven works best.
  2. Add cumin seeds, bay leaves and cardamon pods and fry for 30 seconds or until fragrant.
  3. Add onion and chillis and fry until translucent and slightly brown, about 10 minutes.
  4. Add garlic and ginger and fry for 10 minutes. (lower the heat if necessary, don't burn the garlic!)
  5. Add tomato and cook until it's softened
  6. Add the other spices and cook for another 5 minutes.
  7. Add the frozen spinach and some water, and turn off the heat. Let this sit for a bit and then try it, you may need to add more salt or spices.
  8. Blend with hand blender or food processor (make sure to remove bay leaves and cardamon pods). You may need to add more water. You can also keep it chunky!
  9. Add back the paneer and some greek yoghurt or cream and bring back to temperature
